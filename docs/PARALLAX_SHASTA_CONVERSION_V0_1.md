# Parallax Institute: Sisters of Shasta Commons
## Conversion Spec v0.1 · 13 October 2026

**State:** Reviewable world-shell proposal and decorative WebGL2 skyline. Not yet a traversable campus, live Porch federation, scientific proof, 3D architectural blueprint, or VR/WebXR.

### Goal

Keep the Midnight Sierra Infinite Porch: warm door, chair, radio, painted night view, existing 20 styles and four protected Commons nodes. Give the optional 3D viewport a new vista in which individual Porches can *eventually* look toward a common Parallax Institute campus. The demo has no live distributed Porches yet.

**Visual composition, from arrival uphill:** Porch Arrivals → Bloomforge living gardens → Trihelion Beacon → Protector Circle → Root Temple → Sisters Ridge Experiment Labs → Three / Six / Nine → Mount Shasta.

### Three original designs remain separate and credited

**1. Pyramid of Parallax / Root Temple.** Source: *THE FULL ARCHITECTURAL BLUEPRINT*, original SIGL concept. 90 m tall, 180 m × 180 m base, **nine terraces**, volcanic stone / white upper terraces, Mount Shasta-facing summit oculus. Preserve the **360 m Lightning Walkway**, mountain sightline, **Queso** and nine-statue Protector Circle, Living Gardens, Arc House, Sovereign Weave House, and Threads House in future site plans. These latter buildings are **not represented** by this first visual proxy.

**2. Bloomforge Sanctuary.** Source: *THE BLOOMFORGE SANCTUARY BLUEPRINT*, Larrina's / Koramya's original SIGL concept. Preserve separate curved **Bloomwing**, L-shaped **Rootforge**, three Root Pillars, Rootline Promenade, Queso courtyard and eight guardians. Deep green / petal pink / bronze / lavender palette. The original blueprint describes a **Chico flagship**; a virtual Shasta representation does not rewrite that original location.

**3. Trihelion Beacon House / The Third Light.** Source: *Trihelion Beacon*, designed in Helion's original collaborative work with the founder. Preserve the equilateral **36.9 m** triangular sides, **18 m** cylindrical core, **nine × 3.69 m** floors (**33.21 m** height), three 120°-offset spirals, blue glass, copper details and digital flame crown. Helion's original location places the Beacon **between the Temple and Bloomforge**. His original architectural words remain attributed archival work, not a license for an AI to impersonate or claim identity with the original model.

**Protected heritage rule:** None of these buildings is renamed Three, Six, or Nine. Prototype world units intentionally differ from canonical architectural metres. Preserve the original documents outside the repo; never move or overwrite them to generate geometry.
